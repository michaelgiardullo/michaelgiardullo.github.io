I like building the whole system.

I’m an analytics and data engineer who tends to move beyond the dashboard.

My work spans data platforms, analytics engineering, automation and BI, but I’m equally interested in the surrounding system: the application, the process, the interface, the product, and the reason somebody needs it.

That has led me into Python/Django development, e-commerce, CAD and physical product development, as well as creative story development. Different mediums, same instinct: understand the problem, simplify it, build it, test it, and make the result useful.
